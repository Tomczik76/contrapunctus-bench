# Provenance — results/2026-06-11

How the four `*.report.json` files in this directory were produced, where
they came from, and how to reproduce the tables from them. Cut with
`make release DATE=2026-06-11 RIVALS=fresh`; the carry-vs-rescore
decisions below were made by its coverage and parity gates, not by hand.

## What these files are

Each `*.report.json` is a per-piece **tier-count report** on the
autonomous `single-pick` mode: one row per piece with `total`
(ground-truth events) and the six match-tier counts
([../../methodology/match-tiers.md](../../methodology/match-tiers.md)).
They are the *scored* output, not raw per-event predictions — scoring (the
engine-coupled, normalizer-dependent step) is already baked in, so
aggregating them is pure arithmetic (`harness/score.py`). They were
slimmed to `single-pick` rows only; the source reports also carried
internal per-detector / key-accuracy diagnostic modes that are not part of
this comparison.

`scores.json` and `common_subset.json` are generated by
`harness/score.py` from these four files; do not hand-edit them.

## Why the rivals were re-scored (RIVALS=fresh)

This release includes a **shared tier-classifier change**: Neapolitan
notation synonyms now match at the **exact** tier (`N6` ≡ `bII6` — one
chord in two spellings; a notation identity, distinct from interpretive
conventions like Cad⁶₄/I⁶₄ which remain at the convention tier). The rule
landed simultaneously in the Scala classifier (`WhenInRomeSuite.
classifyEventTier`) and the shared Python classifier
(`music21_comparison.py`, reused by the augnet/analysisgnn scripts), so
**every engine is re-credited symmetrically**: Contrapunctus gained
+0.05pp micro from the rule; AugmentedNet gained +0.05pp micro of its own
(51.46 → 51.51).

The first `make release` attempt (RIVALS=carry) was **refused by the
parity gate** for exactly this reason: the carried 2026-06-10 rival
reports predate the rule, and publishing them against our re-scored
report would have under-credited the rivals. The gate's failure output
prompted the fresh re-score. This is the gate working as designed.

`music21.report.json` is carried forward: Music21 is a fixed external
tool, and the refresh's re-scored music21 numbers are identical to the
carried report (its predictions contain no N↔bII cross-notation events on
the common subset), as verified by the parity gate passing on all four
engines.

## What changed vs results/2026-06-10

Engine gains between the releases (all shipped to the engine repo's main
between 2026-06-10 and 2026-06-11):

- `RnEventTicks` canonical event→tick mapping (two alignment-bug fixes)
  plus a model re-bake on the corrected ticks.
- Brahms-lieder key-detection work (the pseudo-fermata mechanism):
  brahms flipped from a −1.07 loss to a **+9.82 win**, moving the genre
  tally from 6/9 to **7/9**.
- The N6 ≡ bII6 exact-tier rule (symmetric, see above).

Headline movement: genre-balanced **50.17 → 52.25** (+2.08), all-pieces
**58.05 → 59.63** (+1.58 vs the 2026-06-10 release's 58.93 basis note:
the 2026-06-10 README reported 58.93 micro on its own basis; both
releases' numbers are reproducible from their committed reports). Common
subset: 505 pieces, 48,237 → **48,242** events (the tier-classifier
change resolved five previously-unresolvable events into the
intersection).

The same campaign also closed three selection-layer mechanisms as
falsified (tick-level Viterbi decoding, span-structural-bass refiguring,
metric-weighted window support) — documented in the README's negative-
results section and, in full, in the engine repo's
`docs/engine/next-steps.md`.
